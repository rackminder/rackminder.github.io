## Description of Service

RackMinder is a driver-assistance utility that helps you avoid driving a roof-mounted load (such as a bike rack, kayak, cargo box, or ski rack) into an overhead structure like a garage or parking deck. Using a small Bluetooth iBeacon tag attached to your load and the garage locations you save, RackMinder detects when you are returning to a saved garage with a load on the roof and delivers a time-sensitive alert.

RackMinder works together with a separately-purchased RackMinder beacon. Without a RackMinder beacon, the app cannot detect a load.
