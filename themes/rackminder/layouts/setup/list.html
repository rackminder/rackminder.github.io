Welcome to RackMinder

Follow these steps to protect your gear, your garage, and your car.

{{/* What's in the box */}}

What You'll Need

  • ✓ Your RackMinder iBeacon tag
  • ✓ Your iPhone (iOS 15 or later)
  • ✓ The RackMinder app (free on the App Store)
  • ✓ Your gear (bike, kayak, skis, etc.)
{{/* Steps */}}
{{/* Step 1 */}}
1

Purchase/Activate Your iBeacon Tag

RackMinder iBeacon tags are available on Amazon. Search for RackMinder iBeacon Tag or purchase here. Starting at $29.99.

Once your tag arrives, locate the small plastic pull tab on the side of the tag and remove it. This activates the battery. The tag requires no charging, no pairing, and no setup — it begins broadcasting automatically.

{{/* Step 2 */}}
2

Download the RackMinder App

Search for RackMinder on the App Store. The app is free — no account or subscription required.

{{/* Step 3 */}}
3

Attach the Tag to Your Gear

Secure the iBeacon tag directly to your roof rack load — your bike frame, kayak hull, ski bag, or any item you carry on the roof. Use the included mount or zip tie. Place it where it won't be damaged by wind or weather.

{{/* Step 4 */}}
4

Add Your Gear in the App

Open RackMinder and tap Add Gear. Hold your iPhone near the tag — the app will detect it automatically. Give it a name (e.g. "Trek Bike" or "Kayak") and tap Save.

{{/* Step 5 */}}
5

Set Up Your Garage

In the app, tap Add Garage. Drive to your garage entrance and tap Set Location — RackMinder will save the GPS coordinates. Give it a name (e.g. "Home") and tap Save. Repeat for any additional garages.

{{/* Step 6 */}}
6

You're Protected

That's it. RackMinder runs silently in the background. When you approach your garage with gear loaded on your roof, you'll receive an alert on your iPhone, Apple Watch, or CarPlay — before you pull in.

{{/* Tips */}}

Good to Know

  • → The iBeacon tag battery lasts up to 2 years. The app will notify you when it needs replacing.
  • → You can add multiple tags and multiple garages — perfect for families or multiple vehicles.
  • → The tag is weatherproof. Leave it on your gear year-round.
  • → Make sure Bluetooth and Location Services are enabled for RackMinder in your iPhone Settings.
